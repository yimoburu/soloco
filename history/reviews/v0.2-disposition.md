# v0.1 review disposition → v0.2 draft

**Status:** Proposed changes, not an approved new baseline. v0.1 is preserved.

Claude's central diagnosis is sound: the structure is usable, but several decisions have no named owner or testable boundary. The revision closes those gaps without adding another management layer.

## Suggestions deliberately limited

- **No new Steward now.** Process responsibility is real, but existing PM and QA ownership can cover it. Reconsider a conventional operations seat only if scale makes that judgment distinct enough to pass the seat test.
- **No compulsory reviewer rotation.** Independence is from authorship. Rotation is useful for a contested judgment, not every defect retest.
- **No numerical-only gate rule.** Observable categorical conditions are valid. The launch example now names actual checks rather than merely saying reviews were accepted.
- **No blanket “licensed human” rule.** Binding commitments stay with the founder or an explicitly authorized person; specific legal requirements still apply. The spec does not invent a universal professional-licensing requirement.
- **Support can begin within Release.** Activate a separate Support seat only when its judgment earns one.
- **Keep one example.** It now includes authorized pilot exposure, candidate-specific approvals, the security postmortem, and an explicitly unpassed funding gate.

## Choices to revisit after a trial

The reading budgets are tunable starting limits, not research-backed constants. The PM/QA division and default delegation approvers are proposed operating choices; test them for bottlenecks before adding roles. Funding evidence requirements remain financing-case-specific and must be independently reviewed before results are collected.
