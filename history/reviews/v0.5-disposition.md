# v0.4 review disposition → v0.5

**Status:** Incorporated into the current design at the founder's request. This is a design revision, not evidence that the runtime exists. The [review](claude-v0.4.md) assessed the pasted spec, not the converter or history. The seven constitutional articles and company-replacement protocol remain unchanged.

| Finding | Disposition | Smallest correction |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Failed evidence gate has no route | Accept gap; narrow remedy | Gate contracts name a failure owner, safe hold, and bounded retry authority/limit. Preserve measured failures and link prospective revisions. Repairs and authorized retries remain delegated; absent/exhausted retry authority holds or stops that experiment. Only reserved changes or exceptions reach the founder. The missing-delivery replacement exception cannot erase a measured failure. |
| 2. Founder direction lacks a recorder | Accept | The interface captures the original instruction; PM is accountable for its attributed decision record and Intent update before dependent work. Clarify ambiguous or expanded interpretations, without requiring another approval for clear instructions or adding a role. |
| 3. Packet qualification lacks a check | Accept; avoid a new gatekeeper | Packets cite a reserved category and authority reference. Software checks structure; independent outcome review checks escalation quality. Address unnecessary escalation through the existing judgment/charter process, without automatically removing authority and increasing founder work. |
| 4. Professional-review policy is implicit | Accept policy gap; modify remedy | Record founder-approved triggers, independent assessor, and change authority at Frame. Consequential classifications need qualified non-author review; unresolved policy/classification holds affected exposure. The broad trigger list is a company policy option, not a universal requirement. Legally required review stays nonwaivable. |
| 5. Funding evidence may be inadequate | Clarify existing review duty | The independent approver explains why the evidence minimums and disclosed assumptions support the declared financing case and outreach permission. Disclosing a required gap cannot earn Pass. Preserve labeled future scenarios; do not prohibit all assumptions used in a financing thesis. |
| 6. Example obscures focus, exposure order, and independence | Accept clarity fixes | PM recommends the segment focus; the founder authorizes it with the paid envelope. The example applies its own Frame policy and completes required review before paid collection. Distinct author/reviewer instances verify source records. |

## Choices retained

- Keep the replacement safeguards in the current spec: company continuity is a core requirement, independent of the file generator's implementation status.
- Make charter-based author/reviewer identity checks explicit for the future runner. This does not claim prompt files enforce them.
- Keep model diversity, stage-specific funding examples, and QA workload redistribution as trial options. Do not impose another platform requirement, token-only budgets, or an arbitrary outcome-count threshold.
- PM can review Research evidence when qualified and non-author; being a consumer alone does not disqualify it.
- v0.4 already required authorization before paid collection, non-author reviewers, independently approved contracts, and a cold-start failure check. Clarify their presentation rather than adding duplicate rules. The revised example also mentions independent outcome-quality review.

Generator reconciliation adds source attribution, founder-packet starters, a single policy home in Intent with a Frame reference, and unapproved failure/retry and contract-rationale fields. No session runner, automatic packet qualification, policy enforcement, or live replacement is implemented by these templates.
