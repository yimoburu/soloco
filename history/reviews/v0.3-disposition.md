# v0.2 review disposition → v0.3 draft

**Status:** Proposed changes. Earlier versions remain unchanged.

The main new finding is correct: declaring a test before collection does not make its criteria adequate. Someone independent must approve whether the contract can meaningfully fail and whether success supports the permission requested.

## Required findings

| Finding | Disposition | Smallest correction |
|---|---|---|
| 1. No independent gate-contract approval | Accept | A qualified non-author approves the claim, method, thresholds, disqualifiers, and permitted assumptions before collection. Freeze the version; revisions retain old results and require fresh approval. Assumptions are never asserted as facts. |
| 2. Blocked has no exit | Accept, clarify outcome | Name the missing item, owner, deadline, and safe hold. Expiry produces Fail—required evidence/review not delivered, unless an independently approved replacement contract exists. This denies permission; it does not claim the hypothesis was disproved. Ordinary expiry does not automatically require a founder packet. |
| 3. PM/QA process conflicts | Accept | Reviewer assignments come from approved charters. QA cannot independently accept its own contributions; another qualified reviewer checks them. Any party can submit a qualifying packet; software routes it and PM cannot suppress it. PM/QA delegation changes need founder approval or an explicitly designated non-party approver. |
| 4. No non-party block arbiter | Accept | Charters name a qualified non-party arbiter. Neither blocker nor blocked author judges validity. The block stands during adjudication. Uncovered authority, unresolved cross-domain deadlock, and override requests remain founder decisions. |
| 5. Legal review provenance unclear | Accept gap; modify blanket remedy | Every finding is labeled agent-assessed, professional review required, or professionally reviewed, with scope and provenance. Require professional review for applicable requirements, unresolved material uncertainty, or the founder's risk policy. Do not turn every payment or standard web contract into a universal lawyer requirement. |
| 6. Public envelope and recovery self-review | Accept | Specify a separate capped public envelope; Legal/Security assess it and Packet 3 explicitly authorizes it. QA independently verifies Release's authored recovery procedure. |

## Other changes

- Define the governing pack as the exact sections and normative references needed for one assignment. Reading and decision budgets remain tunable ceilings, not a required number of decisions in each spec. Token tracking may supplement them; it need not replace them.
- Instantiation now explicitly includes the Frame reviewer map, ceilings, envelope, and arbiters.
- A stalled PM alerts the founder; routine replacements otherwise retain existing charters and blocks.
- The example gives actual illustrative Solution results, observable Launch results, and an independent outcome classification. It identifies T1 as agent-assessed, with no claim of counsel review.
- The cold-start scenario illustrates a missed cap preventing permission until spec repair.

## Recommendation

Trial this version against a bounded scenario after review of the remaining choices. Observe whether the existing PM/QA split works before adding roles. Findings that appear only as imagined implementation details should not automatically become new governing rules.
