## What these checks establish

separate merged native fictional review fixture establishes actual reviewer
delivery and guarded resume for its exact fixture. Neither proves a live
successor's comprehension or trusted desktop activation.

The new evaluators in this change use disposable fictional companies and existing
production Store, dispatch, worker, sandbox-test, review and acceptance paths.
Their synthetic test grants and contract doubles are labeled explicitly. Native
CLI runs, when selected, retain failures and exact evidence separately; they do
not change an actual company's owner, pause, governance, notices or allowances.

One descriptive paired workload cannot establish statistical non-inferiority,
production latency, savings, reviewer competence or natural founder workload.
No failed run is converted to a pass by a later run.
