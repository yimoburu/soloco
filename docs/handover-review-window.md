# Fresh streamed handover execution windows

v0.6.14 optionally separates an ordinary handover review's total execution window from each native invocation. `company_dispatch` accepts `handover_review_window_minutes: 30 | 45` and a nonempty `handover_review_reason` only for a fresh, current, paused protocol-v2 handover QA target. Both fields are recorded and bound to the exact review context. Applicable authority is still required. Omission preserves the existing total execution timeout and legacy behavior.

`timeout_ms` remains the invocation cap, at most 600,000 ms. The total window begins at the first actual native spawn, never at queue admission or prompt construction. Every call receives at most the smaller of its cap and the remaining fixed window. Each actual spawn is checked, and late/missing call timing holds the result. Existing queue/startup limits, cancellation/termination uncertainty, byte/range limits and same-native full evidence delivery remain unchanged.

Selection requires an unused reconstruction and fresh session reference; it cannot renew a consumed target or extend an existing job. The worker rejects changes to policy, reason, start or deadlines. Reconciliation uses the total window, and resume additionally checks that the original artifact publication and job completion occurred strictly before its recorded deadline. Later resume still needs a current exact-state independent Pass; a completed job alone grants nothing.

This corrects a mismatch for multi-call review: 5.4 million required units need at least 42 four-range delivery packets plus an initial invocation, before extra originals or escaping losses. A fixed ten-minute job lifetime leaves under 14 seconds per invocation in that example. Thirty or 45 minutes supplies review time only, not proof of model fit, comprehension, completion or permission. Choose an adequate supported model and retain a Blocked result when acceptance cannot be earned.

Synthetic clock tests cover successful twelve-minute execution with bounded calls, omitted-window compatibility, unused-target/session admission, immutable timing, shortened first calls, exact expiry, late terminal receipts, later calls shrinking to the remaining allowance and a complete 44-minute simulated review. They do not constitute a real-company Pass or observed 44-minute native run. The independent `resume_handover` gate, previous failures and company governance remain intact. The queued latency candidate is not included.
