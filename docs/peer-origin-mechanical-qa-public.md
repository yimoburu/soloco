# Public controls for pre-quarantine mechanical QA

`company_origin` records preparation through the actual root HOST caller and
current managed-hook route. Every legacy operation checks the original owner
and explicit epoch before private reads, and the fixed writer rechecks inside
its own transaction. It never initializes a company or selects another actor.

The workflow has separate steps:

1. `mechanical_decision` records the complete founder-attributed instruction,
   primary-message locator, exact quote and hash, finite expiry, explicit model
   selection and limits. Attribution does not mechanically authenticate a human.
2. `plan` may bind that exact decision through its closed `mechanical_qa`
   selector. This creates no worker or native allowance.
3. `mechanical_packet` prepares the exact immutable original plan, job template
   and complete assignment context. Oversized context Holds without truncation.
4. `mechanical_packet_inspect` and `mechanical_packet_read` expose the original
   context as historical DATA. Read ranges are at most 4,096 UTF-16 characters,
   preserve surrogate boundaries and carry `next_offset`; encoded replies stay
   within 24,576 bytes. Retrieval does not assert current eligibility.
5. `mechanical_grant` records a separate instruction for that concrete packet.
   Its expiry cannot exceed the original packet or decision deadline.
6. `mechanical_review` requires the exact original `grant_ref` and `plan_id`.
   The engine revalidates the fixed packet, original owner/epoch, finite budget
   and actual execution inventory inside the original queue transaction.

The public tool never accepts a provider, launch callback, actor override,
capacity assertion, native proof or eligibility flag. Decision, packet and grant
writers do not launch workers or consume an assignment attempt. Queue admission
charges the one original attempt with the actual job and retains a SAME-Store
private receipt for its final commit fence. Generic dispatch cannot bypass it.

`mechanical_preflight` and `quarantine` require the source-selected mechanical
purpose and explicit ownership epoch. Public apply uses only
`quarantinePeerOriginAtEpoch`; it cannot fall back to the internal compatibility
writer. Historical-shaped QA or a bare old plan cannot supply the new authority.

Missing fixed exports, undeployed sidecar capture, unknown execution cardinality,
changed original context, a later owner or expired limits remain Holds. Failed or
unknown attempts remain spent. Nothing renews old grants, releases prior UNKNOWN
units, resumes ordinary company work or proves native/whole-domain isolation.

The complete installed team in the retained fictional fit probe exceeded the
existing assignment character limit. Smaller complete fictional fixtures test
software mechanics only; they establish neither installed-team nor real-company
fit. A future lossless, versioned producer must be reviewed independently before
an actual packet or grant can rely on a different context representation.
