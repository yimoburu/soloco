An ordinary role timeout keeps its original verdict, execution failure, scope, expiry and usage. A later process close supplies physical lifecycle evidence only. It cannot turn failed work into a completed artifact, Pass, receiver acceptance, a retry, refund, resume or new grant.

`runModel` accepts paired private software callbacks `onModelProcess(birth)` and `onModelClosure(birth,receipt)`. They are absent from public/native tool arguments. Birth is emitted by the actual original ChildProcess spawn event and binds a fresh launch nonce, actual PID, worker PID, spawn time and digests of the selected command string, argv and cwd. Those digests do not attest executable binary contents or native semantic identity. The original close event is retained after timeout/grace settlement, while output parsing and business timers/listeners are removed. Original stdout/stderr/stdin error observers stay active during draining until actual child close; they cannot replace the timeout or accept late output. Final close removes every retained observer. Only that original ChildProcess close emits the receipt; a signal request, PID absence, callback-shaped public JSON or an earlier read invocation does not.

The worker first writes `job.model_process.requested`, then actual `job.model_process.born`, and finally `job.model_process.closed`. `model_process_tracking` binds each invocation to its original job, role instance, worker and host. Closed read rounds remain distinct from the current invocation, including PID reuse. The closure write commits before separate idempotent reconciliation, so an interrupted second write leaves evidence available. Failed timeout evidence stays byte-for-byte unchanged. Cancelled/interrupted jobs acquire a finish timestamp only when their original process closure is genuinely reconciled.

It accepts no PID, termination Boolean, process receipt, alternate actor or proof label. In normalized layout4 it checks the original requested/born/closed storage events, their raw/hash-exact retained before/after immutable jobs, exact single-field producer transitions and byte-equivalent nontracking job fields in every actual requested/born/closed event, ordered original identity and matching closure. Inventory admission is bounded to 10,000 relevant events for this job, independent of unrelated companies/jobs. Missing or oversized evidence holds. Existing legacy-JSON events lack an attributable immutable job delta, so cold reconciliation remains held there; the still-live private original close callback may finish its own already-observed lifecycle without pretending historical evidence exists. No format relabel or implicit migration occurs.

`company_progress` remains a read-only inspection path. Job previews show process counts and an exact-source reference. `company_status` and `company_wait` may reconcile other lifecycle metadata and do not manufacture process closure. Historical failures produced before this protocol cannot be retrospectively assigned a new process birth or closure.
