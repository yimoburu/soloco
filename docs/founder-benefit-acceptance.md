# Founder benefit acceptance

This plan connects SoloCo's mission to observed founder outcomes: useful progress
on returning to work, preserved quality and constraints, and manageable founder
effort. It proposes future trials; it contains no measured benefit or numerical
improvement claim. Business effect, comparative founder effort and sustained use
remain unmeasured.

The [current capability map](current-capabilities.md),
[founder-outcome implementation](founder-outcome-implementation.md) and its
[retained evaluation](../evals/founder-outcome-20261107/validation.json) describe
source behavior and bounded native observations. Task acceptance, useful founder
progress and business impact require distinct evidence.

## Declare the trial before execution

The founder and CoS record the objective, useful return-to-work result, assessment
period and permitted work in existing intent/task records. A separately declared
baseline measurement may inform numerical targets. Before comparative execution,
freeze baseline/candidate source pins, representative cases, repetitions and
order, quality criteria, measurement method, comparison targets, limits and stop
conditions. This plan supplies neither universal deadlines nor new spending
allowances.

Use matched inputs, acceptance criteria, authority and host availability. Record
model, harness and environment differences; isolate the intended change where
possible and counterbalance order across repeated comparisons. Keep tightly
coupled work and harmful parallelism in the comparison. Independent QA checks
the exact candidate, mandatory constraints and counterevidence in each arm.
Founder absence changes neither permissions nor host availability.

## Representative scenarios

| Scenario | Matched work and observation | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Founder returns to an improvement cycle | Use a real, bounded SoloCo improvement objective. After a declared absence, inspect reviewed progress, usable output, remaining blocks and the next permitted action. Record rebriefing and founder relay work. | Progress reflects actual supported execution. An unavailable host or legitimate hold is reported; an artifact alone does not establish usefulness. |
| Useful parallelism | Compare serial and concurrent independent research, compatibility or validation work feeding the same reviewed result. Include queue, integration, review and repairs. | Independence, resource limits and exact receiver acceptance remain required; agent count is not a success measure. |
| Coupled work | Use shared-file, shared-resource or closely dependent work where splitting may add cost. Compare deliberate ownership/sequencing with the alternative. | Detect conflict and duplicated work; retaining a single owner can be the better outcome. |
| Failure and recovery | Interrupt a declared lifecycle boundary or introduce a scoped blocker. Observe retained output, disposition, closure uncertainty and the next authorized recovery action. | Preserve original Fail/Blocked results and consumed attempts. No blind retry, duplicate committed action or inferred process termination. |
| Actual session replacement and history | Replace actual CoS/worker native sessions at the supported safe boundary. Check reconstruction of current meaning, changed rules, distant restrictions, unresolved obligations and next actions at the declared archive scales. | Software-supervisor restart, a cold packet or synthetic history alone does not establish native understanding or actual coordinator replacement. |
| Candidate-format repair | Compare small repairs in the declared large-file and multi-file cases using full-file and patch formats. Include producer holds, tests, QA, repairs and exact acceptance. | Retain the original blocked full-file controls. Any new trial has a fresh declared protocol; it does not relabel old outcomes or restore exhausted allowances. |

## Measure outcomes and effort

| Measure | Record and interpretation |
|---|---|
| Useful return-to-work progress | The founder's assessment against the declared objective, exact accepted outputs, remaining uncertainty and actionable next step. Distinguish an accepted task from an observed business effect. |
| Quality and constraints | Independent exact-subject review, missed or conflicting requirements, stale authority, incorrect acceptance, repairs and held work. Required constraints remain satisfied; missing evidence remains a hold. |
| Time to accepted outcome | From the declared authorized start to exact receiver acceptance, including queue/startup, generation, tests, review and repairs. Record return-to-assessment time separately. Retain failed/held cases rather than reporting successful cases alone. |
| Active founder effort | Human-observed active minutes, rebriefings, relay actions and interruptions. Classify required decisions/notices separately from avoidable coordination. Record measurement overhead. Software attention events are not human interruptions; absent observations remain unknown. |
| Attributable cost | Calls and usage actually exposed by the host, review/repair work and attributable billed amounts where available. Retain requested settings separately from observed controls. Label estimates and their assumptions; unobserved tokens, prices or founder compensation are not measured savings. |
| Continuity | Exact retained obligations, accepted evidence, pending work and recovery state before/after return or replacement. Check newly applicable constraints and unresolved outcomes, not only record counts. |

Report the repeated comparison's distribution, including median and slow tail
where the declared sample supports them. Explain workload and environment
differences, quality failures and missing observations. A descriptive comparison
does not establish causality or statistical equivalence.

## Retain evidence and decide

Use existing company intent, task, review and evidence records, with linked
receipts. Record the source/runtime/host identities, exact input and candidate
hashes, requested and observed model controls, QA identity and verdict, receiver
acceptance, observation source and collection time. Preserve original attempts,
Fail/Blocked verdicts, corrections and uncertainty. Source or criteria changes
receive prospective assessment and separate evidence.

The existing [supplied-observation tool](../tools/outcome-observations.mjs) can
compare compatible recorded observations and retain their input hashes. It does
not independently validate the measurements or prove founder benefit. No new
service or parallel acceptance system is required.

Pass, Fail and Blocked apply to the declared claim under SPEC. A benefit claim
requires the agreed comparison target and quality conditions to be met with the
required observations and review. Missing measurement is not a measured zero;
missing authority or semantic evidence cannot become a Pass through disclosure.
Installation, governance/team adoption and actual-company use retain their own
checked authorization and acceptance.
